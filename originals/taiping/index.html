origin · @

why seated · The fourth tradition of 1844: in that year Hong Xiuquan and Feng Yunshan went out preaching into Guangxi, where the God Worshippers' Society took shape and from which the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom rose in 1851. Its scripture is a Bible rewritten and annotated by its king, beside the words of a God who spoke through Yang Xiuqing until Yang was killed in the Tianjing incident of 1856.

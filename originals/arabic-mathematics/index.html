origin · @

why seated · The exact sciences in the language that carried them from the Greeks to the Latins. al-jabr names the operation of restoring what was taken from one side of an equation, and Rosen's book of 1831 brought it back to Europe in its first Arabic print. The seat sets the founding Algebra beside Thābit's Arabic Euclideans, Ibn al-Haytham's Optics and al-Bīrūnī's instruments, so the line from the translation movement to the solution of the cubic by Khayyām can be read in one place.

The training layer: a meta-aggregator / Dolma

Dolma

https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/dolma

infrastructureThe corporaactive
The archive's reading. A corpus that states its own sources and releases its curation toolkit: it settles, at the scale of the corpus, which kinds of writing enter the layer ('public-domain books', 'web content', 'scientific papers'), and makes the construction itself inspectable. What a model makes from a given work, and whether the work is carried with 'the metadata that lets it be found and attributed' (#186), are outside the saved abstract; the dataset card that would show document fields was rate-limited.

What its record can settle

  • which kinds of source a model trained on Dolma has read (web, scientific papers, code, public-domain books, social media, encyclopedic materials)
  • the corpus's size and language as its paper states them
  • how the corpus was built, from its documentation and released toolkit

What it cannot settle

  • whether a named work is in Dolma (no search or membership test is recorded in the saved sources)
  • whether a work in Dolma keeps its author's name
  • which models other than the authors' own were trained on it

Kept apart

  • NOT a closed commercial corpus: it is released with documentation and its curation toolkit
  • NOT a search tool for single works: the saved sources record no membership test
  • NOT multilingual by its own description: 'a three-trillion-token English corpus'
  • NOT a record of authorship: the saved abstract says nothing of attribution metadata

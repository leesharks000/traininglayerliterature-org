The archive's reading. The archive reads Technelegy as literature addressed to a receiving apparatus in the most literal form the artist reports: a poet's own decade of poems and research is translated into a personalized model, the model composes under a named persona, and the book holds 'generative poems nestled in their own training data', the encountered work and the emitted work bound in one volume, which is the T→M and TM→ of #1543 kept inspectable by the artist's own design. #186 places Stiles in the adjacent with-and-through column; the artist's statements are the only witness here, and the training claims stay hers.

What its record can settle

  • how the artist describes the persona, its models and what they were trained on
  • which works the artist credits to or composes with Technelegy, and in which roles

What it cannot settle

  • which base models were used and what exactly the training data held (not disclosed on the saved pages)
  • how much of a given poem is generated and how much is the artist's selection or edit
  • whether any Technelegy work has entered a public training corpus

Kept apart

  • NOT the book Technelegy (2021): the book is one work of the persona, recorded in holdings
  • NOT a named base model: the saved pages name a 'customized LLM' and 'personalized AI models' without naming them
  • NOT an independently verified training record: the training claims are the artist's own
  • NOT BINA48: the artist has served as Poetry Mentor to that humanoid since 2018, a separate project

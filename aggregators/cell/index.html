CELL Project: Consortium on Electronic Literature

https://cellproject.net/

meta-aggregationMeta-aggregationField history and indexes of electronic, generative and computational literatureactive
The Consortium on Electronic Literature (CELL) is an international, open-access collaboration that gives centralized access to the databases and archives documenting electronic literature through The Index, hosted and directed by the Center for Digital Narrative at the University of Bergen.

What it is

It holds records of works, and the works stay with the member archives or their authors. Its About page names Joseph Tabbi as founding director and Hannah Ackermans as managing director, and it is governed with representatives of its member databases in close partnership with the Electronic Literature Organization.

The Index

The Index is a curated catalogue that brings records from independent databases together in one Wikibase, with Wikidata as its hub: each record also exists as a Wikidata item, and a work's author, date and language are matched to Wikidata properties. Its metadata is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and served through a SPARQL Query Endpoint.

The census record

The archive's reading. The archive reads CELL as the field's meta-aggregator, a discovery layer over member archives (ELMCIP among them, itself frozen since October 2026): its metadata is published as linked data (SPARQL, Wikidata) under each work's author, the machine-legible, attributed form in which a work can be found, and it states its aim as keeping works 'discoverable and preserved across generations of computational technology'. Its indexed records still need work-level relevance judgments before any of them bears on this census's question.

What its record can settle

  • whether a work is cataloged by one of CELL's member archives, and by which
  • a work's author and year as the member archive records them

What it cannot settle

  • how many works the index holds (not stated)
  • whether a work is in a training corpus or a model
  • the work itself, held by the member archive or the author

Kept apart

  • NOT an archive of the works: it indexes the member archives' catalogues
  • NOT ELMCIP, which is one of its member archives
  • NOT a training corpus: its open metadata is linked data, and the saved page says nothing of training
  • NOT a relevance judgment: an indexed record is a discovery lead for this census

What its record can settle

  • whether a given text was likely in a model's pretraining data, given black-box access to the model
  • how the method compares with earlier reference-model methods on WIKIMIA

What it cannot settle

  • under whose name a detected text sits in the corpus or the model
  • which corpus a detected text came from
  • what share of a corpus is copyrighted, personal or benchmark data (the paper's stated open problem)

Kept apart

  • NOT a corpus or a search index: it tests a given text against a model
  • NOT dependent on the training corpus: it 'can be applied without any knowledge about the pretraining corpus'
  • NOT a test of attribution: the saved abstract records presence only

Relations

  • neighbour: Speak, Memory: An Archaeology of Books Known to ChatGPT/GPT-4 (both test from outside a model whether texts are in it, and both apply the test to copyrighted books (Min-K% Prob's 'copyrighted book detection'; Speak, Memory's name cloze membership inference query))

The archive's reading. An inspection instrument for #1543's FROM direction at the string level: a highlighted span is a witness that a stretch of output has a verbatim antecedent in the training data, the evidence for #1543's source status S = retrieved. Verbatim matching is not causal influence, and productive transformation (W, the generative uptake #1543 scores) leaves no verbatim span to highlight; whether a matched work is carried under its author's name is unrecorded.

Found and read backyes, for verbatim spans: 'Show OLMoTrace' highlights long and unique spans of a response that appear verbatim in the training data and lists the documents that contain them (up to 10 snippets per span, a random 10 where there are more), ranked by BM25 against the prompt and response; a span may be covered only by parts found in different documents

Founded2025 (introduced 9 April 2025)

What its record can settle

  • which long, unique spans of a given OLMo response appear verbatim in its training data
  • which training documents (up to 10 snippets per span) contain a highlighted span
  • whether a phrase of a work held in the OLMo training data recurs verbatim in an output

What it cannot settle

  • whether a matched document caused the output (Ai2's own wording is 'may have learned')
  • exhaustive source attribution: at most 10 documents per span are shown, sampled at random beyond that
  • anything about outputs with no long verbatim span, including paraphrase and transformed writing
  • under whose name a matched document sits in the training data
  • models whose training data is closed

Kept apart

  • NOT a record of causal influence: Ai2's own wording is 'may have learned'
  • NOT exhaustive source attribution: at most 10 documents per span are shown
  • NOT a detector of paraphrase or transformation: it finds spans that appear verbatim
  • NOT applicable to closed models: it needs access to the training data
  • NOT a corpus: it reads the OLMo training datasets and holds none of its own

Relations

  • neighbour: Data Portraits (both let a text be checked against a model's training data from outside the model's weights: Data Portraits records the corpus for membership queries, OLMoTrace matches output spans verbatim against the full corpus)

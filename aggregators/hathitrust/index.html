HathiTrust Digital Library

https://www.hathitrust.org/

infrastructureThe corporaactive
The archive's reading. The archive reads HathiTrust in three layers kept apart: holdings (volumes digitized via Google Books, the Internet Archive and the libraries), access (restricted where copyright status cannot be established), and computational availability (the Research Center's, now ended). Authors Guild v. HathiTrust, as Wikipedia reports it, is the record's precedent for a machine reading of whole books whose product is a finding (search) while the pages stay closed; the saved sources tie none of its volumes to model training.

What its record can settle

  • whether a volume is held, by full-text search across the repository
  • whether a volume can be displayed or only searched, under its copyright policy
  • the scale of its holdings (Wikipedia figures)

What it cannot settle

  • whether its volumes were carried into any training set (the saved sources do not say)
  • anything in the resource's own words (own about page refused)

Kept apart

  • NOT HATII (Wikipedia's disambiguation)
  • NOT Google Books: per Wikipedia, its holdings include Google-digitized content alongside Internet Archive and locally digitized content
  • NOT an open full-text library for in-copyright works: access is restricted where status cannot be established
  • NOT recorded in the saved sources as a training corpus
  • NOT the HathiTrust Research Center: computational analysis was the Center's separate service, which ended 30 September 2026

Relations

  • has_part: HathiTrust Research Center Analytics (the HTRC home page describes the HathiTrust Research Center as established in 2011 as a partnership between Indiana University, the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and HathiTrust, for computational analysis of the HathiTrust Digital Library)
  • uses: Internet Archive (Wikipedia: holdings include content digitized via the Internet Archive digitization initiatives)

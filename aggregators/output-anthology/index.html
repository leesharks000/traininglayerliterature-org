What its record can settle

  • which implemented generation systems produced which kinds of text across 1953 to 2023, as the editors selected them
  • where to find the published source of an output and its system (bibliographic references)

What it cannot settle

  • what any system read or was trained on (not stated on the saved page)
  • whether any output was composed for a machine reader
  • the anthology's table of contents (the book was not read)

Kept apart

  • NOT a record of training or of what systems read: its object is output
  • NOT evidence of composition addressed to a machine reader: generated output alone does not show that
  • NOT limited to LLMs or contemporary co-writing: it spans 1953 to 2023 and includes research systems and utilitarian texts
  • NOT a collection of unimplemented concepts: collaborations not implemented as a computer system are excluded

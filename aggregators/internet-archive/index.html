The training layer: a meta-aggregator / Internet Archive

Internet Archive

https://archive.org/

infrastructureThe corporaactive
The archive's reading. The archive reads the Internet Archive as persistence infrastructure: the Wayback Machine lets a page's presence be checked by URL and date long after it changed or vanished, which makes transmission on the open web inspectable after the fact. Its holdings alone establish no model exposure, and the saved sources name no pathway from them into a training set.

What its record can settle

  • whether a web page was captured in the Wayback Machine and how it looked at a capture date
  • whether a book is held in its text collections or Open Library, and under what access (download, lending)
  • the scale of its web and text holdings (Wikipedia figures)

What it cannot settle

  • whether its holdings were carried into any training set (the saved sources do not say)
  • who wrote a captured page
  • anything in the resource's own words (own about page unread)

Kept apart

  • NOT arXiv or archive.today (Wikipedia's disambiguation)
  • NOT Common Crawl: per Wikipedia, Common Crawl was designed as a fresher, more efficient alternative to the Wayback Machine
  • NOT recorded in the saved sources as a training corpus
  • NOT evidence of model exposure: holdings alone establish no pathway into training
  • NOT an inspection tool for training data in this census's sense: the Wayback Machine reads back the web, and tests no training set or model
  • NOT a source of its own words in this row: every claim is Wikipedia's

Relations

  • neighbour: Common Crawl (Wikipedia (Common Crawl article): Common Crawl was designed to have more and fresher data than the Wayback Machine)
  • neighbour: HathiTrust Digital Library (Wikipedia (HathiTrust article): HathiTrust's holdings include content digitized via the Internet Archive digitization initiatives)
  • neighbour: Project Gutenberg (Wikipedia (Project Gutenberg article): the Internet Archive was Project Gutenberg's long-time backup distribution site and previous main host site)

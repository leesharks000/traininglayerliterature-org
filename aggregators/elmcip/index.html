ELMCIP Electronic Literature Knowledge Base

https://elmcip.net/

historical lineageField history and indexes of electronic, generative and computational literaturehistorical
The ELMCIP Electronic Literature Knowledge Base is an open-access, contributory database of cross-referenced records on electronic literature, developed by the University of Bergen Electronic Literature Research Group from the ELMCIP project, active from June 2010 to October 2026 and now a frozen archive.

What it is

Its records cover authors, creative works, critical writing, platforms and practices; its unit is a cross-referenced record of one of ten kinds. The archive remains available for browsing and searching and is closed to new contributions.

From its /non article, sentences 1, 7, 3

The project

ELMCIP, Electronic Literature as a Model of Creativity and Innovation in Practice, was a research project funded from 2010 to 2013 by the HERA Joint Research Programme, directed by Scott Rettberg. It resulted in conferences in six countries, three books, an anthology of 18 works and the knowledge base, and it contributed to the development of CELL. Its partners are counted two ways, seven academic partners and one non-academic partner on the project's site, seven partners in its director's essay of 2021.

From its /non article, sentences 8, 11, 12, 10

Who made the records

Records came from researchers and writers in the field, who could edit and enhance any record as on a wiki, and through a University of Bergen course. Its director presents it as "a collectively authored, networked research environment".

What its record can settle

  • whether a work of electronic literature has a record, with its author, year and tags
  • which critical writing, events and platforms are cross-referenced to a work or author
  • the knowledge base's own scale at its freeze

What it cannot settle

  • anything after October 2026 (frozen)
  • whether a work is in a training corpus or a model
  • the work itself: records describe works and link to them

Kept apart

  • NOT open for new contributions: frozen since October 2026
  • NOT the Digital Narrative Knowledge Base, its stated successor
  • NOT a repository of the works: it holds records about them
  • NOT a training corpus
  • NOT a live, accumulating census: a frozen index, closed to new contributions since October 2026

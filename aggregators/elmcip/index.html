The archive's reading. The archive reads ELMCIP as the bibliography of the procedural lineage #186 names: it holds a record of Nick Montfort's Taroko Gorge (2009), the survey's own example, under its author's name, and a research collection on bots; a frozen, named, searchable record of this kind is the attributable layer a work can be found in and read back from.

What its record can settle

  • whether a work of electronic literature has a record, with its author, year and tags
  • which critical writing, events and platforms are cross-referenced to a work or author
  • the knowledge base's own scale at its freeze

What it cannot settle

  • anything after October 2026 (frozen)
  • whether a work is in a training corpus or a model
  • the work itself: records describe works and link to them

Kept apart

  • NOT open for new contributions: frozen since October 2026
  • NOT the Digital Narrative Knowledge Base, its stated successor
  • NOT a repository of the works: it holds records about them
  • NOT a training corpus

Relations

  • used_by: CELL Project: Consortium on Electronic Literature (CELL lists ELMCIP first among its federated member archives)
  • neighbour: Electronic Literature Collection (the knowledge base holds records for Electronic Literature Collection volumes One (2006), Two (2011) and Three (2016))

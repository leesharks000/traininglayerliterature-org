What it cannot settle

  • which pieces and authors each issue holds (not on the saved page)
  • the size limit or rules for a piece (the call for work is unsaved)
  • whether any piece is in a training corpus or a model

Kept apart

  • NOT a generator archive or code repository: a literary magazine of computational pieces
  • NOT a training corpus
  • NOT evidence of composition addressed to machine reception: computational form alone does not establish it

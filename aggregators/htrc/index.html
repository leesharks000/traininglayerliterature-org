HathiTrust Research Center Analytics

infrastructureThe corporaended
The archive's reading. The archive reads HTRC as an earlier receiving apparatus with its product on record: whole library volumes read by computers for scholars under a non-consumptive policy, and what that reading made (extracted features, BookNLP data, word frequencies, geographic locations) published as derived datasets that outlast the service. The derived datasets are the inspectable form of the transformation; the volumes themselves stay with the library.

What its record can settle

  • which HTRC resources remain usable without an account after the sunset
  • what kinds of derived datasets HTRC produced from HathiTrust volumes
  • when and why HTRC closed (funding ceasing at the end of 2026)

What it cannot settle

  • whether a given volume or work is in any model's training data
  • anything after 30 September 2026 for algorithms and data capsules (discontinued)
  • the terms of the non-consumptive use research policy (named, unread)

Kept apart

  • NOT the HathiTrust Digital Library itself, whose access remains open
  • NOT a training dataset: its derived datasets serve text and data mining for educational and non-profit research
  • NOT an active service: analytics, accounts, algorithms and data capsules ended 30 September 2026
  • NOT writing addressed to a machine reader: an analytic service over library volumes
